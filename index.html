Our Approach

A Thoughtful Journey From Concept to Celebration

Every bespoke celebration and custom mug collection follows our disciplined four-step pathway, ensuring peace of mind at every turn.

01

Consult

An attentive initial conversation to understand your personal aesthetic, event requirements, and bespoke mug vision.

02

Concept

Developing tailored design palettes, spatial layout drafts, and custom mug artwork proofs for your approval.

03

Create

Meticulous fabrication, floral styling prep, and precision printing of your signature event mugs.

04

Coordinate

Meet the Founder

Founder & Director

Marie Ange Melin

British-Ivorian Entrepreneur & Businesswoman

Marie Ange Melin is a British-Ivorian entrepreneur and businesswoman with more than 25 years' experience in food services and hospitality. Discipline, customer care, teamwork and responsibility shape everything she builds. She leads MAFA Amazing Peace Ltd, is Founder and CEO of Praise for Jesus Gospel Concert Ltd, is the author of three books, and holds a BA in Business Studies with Entrepreneurship (UKCBC and University of West London).
